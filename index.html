About Us

InAmigos Foundation is a trusted NGO established on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, dedicated to creating meaningful social impact across multiple domains.

We work tirelessly to support the underprivileged, empower women, protect the environment, and develop youth skills. Our organization holds prestigious certifications including 80G, 12A, CSR-1, NITI Aayog recognition, and ISO 9001:2015 accreditation.

Our Impact

Through our diverse programs, we are making a tangible difference in society. Our holistic approach addresses multiple social challenges simultaneously—from poverty and education gaps to environmental conservation and skills development.

Our Mission

To uplift underserved communities through targeted interventions in education, healthcare, economic empowerment, and environmental sustainability.

Our Vision

To build a society where every individual has access to opportunities for growth, dignity, and sustainable livelihood.
